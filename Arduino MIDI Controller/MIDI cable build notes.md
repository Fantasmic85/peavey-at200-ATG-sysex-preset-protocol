MIDI cable build notes

As seen in the schamatic, fou sending MIDI instructions only, a standard MIDI cable can be used.
You will only need a Y cable to retrieve preset information from the guitar. The Y cable can be made with 5 Pin plugs.
An 8 Pin plug is only necessary for phantom power and audio out.

1 - Options are given for female MIDI ports (panel mount and extension cables) and male MIDI plugs.
2 - Mind your arduino voltage.
3 - Antares reports that using pin 8 for audio output may cause artifacts in the audio signal. Test first. Beware og Ground Loops.
4 - Do not power your guitar via the MIDI port without verify the pinout. You will damage your guitar if 6V and GND are switched.
